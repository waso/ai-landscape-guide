    The AI landscape field guide¶

    About 340 AI and machine-learning terms, explained in plain language and placed on a map of where they sit: pre-training, post-training, fine-tuning, inference, applications.

    Made for people who learn from videos and courses and keep meeting new acronyms. Every entry has a short definition and a why it matters line; seven maps show how the pieces fit together.

    • Heard a term?

      Use the search box or the A–Z index. Every acronym from the videos is there, including the ones that are only nicknames.

    • Want the big picture?

      Start with the seven maps: the nested circles of AI, the LLM lifecycle, the training loop, the life of one request, inside a Transformer block, RAG and agents, and a decision guide.

    • Want it explained?

      The glossary has 14 sections ordered like the lifecycle, so neighbours on the map are neighbours on the page.

    • Found a mistake, or a missing term?

      This is a community document. Open an issue or a pull request — see how to contribute.

    The 14 sections¶

    # Section What it covers
    1 The big picture How AI, ML, deep learning and LLMs relate
    2 Learning paradigms How a model learns: from labels, from structure, from itself, or from rewards
    3 Classic ML toolbox Pre-deep-learning algorithms and the vocabulary of any ML project
    4 Neural-network foundations The training loop and building blocks used by every stage that changes weights
    5 Data & tokenization Stage 1 of the LLM lifecycle: what the model reads, and in what units
    6 The Transformer architecture Stage 2: the shape of the network before any weights are learned
    7 Pre-training Stage 3: the expensive phase that produces a base model
    8 Post-training & alignment Stage 4: turning a base model into an assistant
    9 Adapting a model to your task Stage 5: your own fine-tuning on an open-weight or API model
    10 Inference & serving Stage 6: running the model to produce tokens, fast and cheaply
    11 Building applications Stage 7: prompting, retrieval, tools and agents around a frozen model
    12 Evaluation, safety & interpretability Cross-cutting: measuring, attacking and understanding models at every stage
    13 Infrastructure & ecosystem Cross-cutting: the hardware and software everything runs on
    14 Beyond text: vision, audio, video Cross-cutting: terms from image, speech and video AI that appear in general AI videos

    Sections 1–4 are the foundations, 5–11 follow the seven stages of the LLM lifecycle (see Map 2), and 12–14 cut across all stages.
